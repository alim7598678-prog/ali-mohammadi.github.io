A little about my work

Small structures.
Meaningful impact.

I am a materials engineering researcher with research interests in metal matrix composites, powder metallurgy, surface engineering, tribology, protective coatings, and advanced ceramics. My research explores the relationships between processing conditions, microstructure, and material performance, including mechanical properties, wear behavior, and corrosion resistance.

My work spans aluminum-based composites reinforced with ceramic nanoparticles, hybrid surface coatings, solid-state processing, and sustainable materials development. I am particularly dedicated to designing and characterizing advanced materials with superior mechanical, tribological, and functional performance.

02 / Research in practice

Research experience

/ 01 — COMPOSITES

Aluminum-Based Metal Matrix Composites

Research on A380 aluminum composites reinforced with SiC nanoparticles using powder metallurgy, focusing on processing conditions, microstructure, hardness, mechanical properties, and wear behavior.

/ 02 — SURFACES

Surface Engineering and Protective Coatings

Research on hybrid coatings and nanoparticle-reinforced coating systems, with an emphasis on tribological performance, corrosion resistance, and multi-property optimization.

/ 03 — PROCESSING

Solid-State Processing and Advanced Materials

Research involving friction stir processing of aluminum alloys, as well as the synthesis and characterization of advanced ceramic and sustainable materials.
