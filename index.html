Ali Mohammadi

Materials Engineering Researcher | Materials Science and Surface Engineering

I am a materials engineering researcher with research interests in metal matrix composites, powder metallurgy, surface engineering, tribology, protective coatings, and advanced ceramics. My research has explored the relationships between processing conditions, microstructure, and material performance, including mechanical properties, wear behavior, and corrosion resistance.

My work spans aluminum-based composites reinforced with ceramic nanoparticles, hybrid surface coatings, solid-state processing, and sustainable materials development. I am particularly interested in developing and characterizing advanced materials with improved mechanical, tribological, and corrosion performance.
